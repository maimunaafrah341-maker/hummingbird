# Safety Data Sheet — Acetone

## Substance name
Acetone (Propan-2-one; CAS 67-64-1)

## Reactivity hazards
Highly flammable liquid and vapor. Vapors may travel to an ignition source and flash back. Forms explosive mixtures with air and reacts dangerously with strong oxidizers. May attack some plastics, rubber, and coatings. Containers can build pressure when heated.

## First Aid
- **Inhalation:** Move the person to fresh air. Keep at rest and monitor breathing. Get medical advice if symptoms persist.
- **Skin contact:** Remove contaminated clothing and wash skin thoroughly with soap and water.
- **Eye contact:** Rinse cautiously with clean water for at least 15 minutes and seek medical attention if irritation continues.
- **Ingestion:** Do not induce vomiting. Call a poison center or physician immediately.

## Mandatory PPE
Chemical splash goggles, solvent-resistant gloves, antistatic protective clothing, and enclosed safety footwear. Use local exhaust ventilation. Select an organic-vapor respirator only when engineering controls cannot maintain exposure below applicable limits.

## Fire-fighting notes
Use alcohol-resistant foam, dry chemical, or carbon dioxide. Water spray may cool containers but may be ineffective for extinguishing the liquid. Eliminate ignition sources, ground and bond transfer equipment, and wear positive-pressure self-contained breathing apparatus in a fire area.
