# Safety Data Sheet — Sulfuric Acid

## Substance name
Sulfuric Acid (H2SO4; concentrated industrial grade)

## Reactivity hazards
Strong acid and powerful dehydrating agent. Violent, heat-producing reaction with water; always add acid slowly to water, never water to acid. Reacts violently with bases, organic materials, reducing agents, and many metals. May produce flammable hydrogen and toxic sulfur oxides during reactions or fire.

## First Aid
- **Skin or eye contact:** Immediately flush with large amounts of water for at least 20 minutes. Remove contaminated clothing during rinsing. Obtain emergency medical attention.
- **Inhalation:** Move to fresh air only if the area is safe. Keep the person warm and at rest; seek immediate medical evaluation.
- **Ingestion:** Do not induce vomiting or give neutralizing agents. Rinse mouth and obtain emergency medical care.

## Mandatory PPE
Acid-rated chemical goggles with face shield, acid-resistant gloves, full chemical suit or apron, and chemical-resistant boots. Use corrosion-resistant local exhaust and a suitable acid-gas respirator only within a formal respiratory-protection program.

## Fire-fighting notes
Not combustible, but it intensifies some fires and reacts with water or combustibles. Use media suitable for surrounding materials. Cool exposed containers from maximum distance with water spray; contain corrosive runoff. Firefighters require full protective clothing and positive-pressure self-contained breathing apparatus.
