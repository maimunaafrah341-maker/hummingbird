# Demo Regulatory Snippet — Factories Act-Style Emergency Preparedness

**On-site emergency arrangements.** The occupier shall provide and maintain an effective arrangement for raising an alarm, isolating process equipment, evacuating affected work areas, and obtaining prompt first aid following a chemical incident. Emergency routes and assembly points shall remain unobstructed and shall be communicated to every worker, contractor, and visitor.

**Chemical handling controls.** Containers and process lines shall be identified, and current hazard information shall be available to persons who may be exposed. Where a corrosive, toxic, or flammable substance is handled, suitable protective clothing, washing facilities, and emergency decontamination equipment shall be provided. Incidents and near misses shall be recorded, reviewed by the site safety committee, and followed by corrective action before normal operations resume.
