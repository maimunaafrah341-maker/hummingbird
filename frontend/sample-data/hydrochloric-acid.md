# Safety Data Sheet — Hydrochloric Acid

## Substance name
Hydrochloric Acid (Hydrogen Chloride solution; HCl, typically 30–37% w/w)

## Reactivity hazards
Strongly corrosive and reactive with bases, carbonates, many metals, and oxidizing agents. Contact with metals may generate flammable hydrogen gas. Mixing with hypochlorites or bleach can release toxic chlorine gas. Reacts exothermically with water when concentrated; always add acid to water under controlled conditions.

## First Aid
- **Inhalation:** Move the person to fresh air without entering the vapor area. Get immediate medical attention if coughing, breathing difficulty, or chest tightness occurs.
- **Skin or eye contact:** Immediately flush with running water for at least 15 minutes. Remove contaminated clothing while rinsing. Obtain urgent medical evaluation.
- **Ingestion:** Do not induce vomiting. Rinse mouth and seek emergency medical care.

## Mandatory PPE
Chemical splash goggles with face shield, acid-resistant gloves, long-sleeved chemical apron or suit, and enclosed chemical-resistant footwear. Use an approved respirator only under a documented respiratory-protection program.

## Fire-fighting notes
Hydrochloric acid is not itself combustible. Cool exposed containers with water fog from a safe distance, but do not direct water into a concentrated acid spill. Prevent corrosive runoff from entering drains. Firefighters require full protective clothing and positive-pressure self-contained breathing apparatus.
