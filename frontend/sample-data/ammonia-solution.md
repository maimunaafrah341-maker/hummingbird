# Safety Data Sheet — Ammonia Solution

## Substance name
Ammonia Solution (Ammonium hydroxide; NH4OH, industrial aqueous solution)

## Reactivity hazards
Corrosive and irritating alkaline solution. Releases pungent ammonia vapor, especially when heated or mixed with acids. Mixing with chlorine-containing products can generate toxic chloramine gases. May corrode copper, brass, zinc, and galvanized surfaces.

## First Aid
- **Inhalation:** Move to fresh air without entering the vapor cloud. Keep airway clear and obtain urgent medical care for breathing symptoms.
- **Skin contact:** Remove contaminated clothing and flush with water for at least 15 minutes.
- **Eye contact:** Rinse continuously for at least 15 minutes and seek immediate medical attention.
- **Ingestion:** Rinse mouth. Do not induce vomiting. Contact emergency medical services.

## Mandatory PPE
Chemical splash goggles and face shield, ammonia-resistant gloves, chemical apron or suit, and chemical-resistant boots. Use local exhaust or supplied-air respiratory protection when vapor levels are unknown or elevated.

## Fire-fighting notes
Aqueous solution is not normally combustible, but released ammonia gas can burn in air. Use water spray, dry chemical, or foam for surrounding fires. Cool containers from a protected position and isolate the release area upwind.
