# Safety Data Sheet — Caustic Soda

## Substance name
Caustic Soda (Sodium Hydroxide; NaOH, solid or concentrated solution)

## Reactivity hazards
Highly corrosive to skin, eyes, and many metals. Dissolving or diluting in water releases substantial heat and can cause violent boiling and splashing. Reacts vigorously with acids, ammonium compounds, and some organic materials. Contact with aluminum, zinc, or tin may produce flammable hydrogen gas.

## First Aid
- **Skin contact:** Remove contaminated clothing and immediately flush the affected area with water for at least 20 minutes. Obtain urgent medical attention.
- **Eye contact:** Rinse continuously with clean water for at least 20 minutes and arrange immediate ophthalmic evaluation.
- **Inhalation:** Move to fresh air. Seek medical care if irritation or breathing difficulty develops.
- **Ingestion:** Do not induce vomiting. Rinse mouth; never give anything by mouth to an unconscious person. Obtain emergency treatment.

## Mandatory PPE
Chemical splash goggles plus face shield, alkali-resistant gloves, full-length chemical suit or apron, and chemical-resistant boots. Use suitable ventilation and avoid dust or mist generation.

## Fire-fighting notes
Non-combustible, but contact with certain metals can generate flammable hydrogen. Use extinguishing media appropriate to the surrounding fire. Cool nearby containers with water spray from a protected position; do not apply water directly to a dry solid spill or concentrated product.
